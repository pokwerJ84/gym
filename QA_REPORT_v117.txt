Gym Tracker Pro v117 — QA REPORT
Date: 2026-08-28

BUG REPRODUCED FROM IPHONE SCREENSHOT
- Device date: Friday 28 August 2026.
- Calendar heading: 24 Aug – 30 Aug.
- Visible calendar row incorrectly remained on 10–15 Aug.
- Last completed Friday (14 Aug) therefore looked like the selected/current workout.

ROOT CAUSES
- The Today screen restored gymSelectedWorkoutDate from localStorage on startup.
- iOS can restore the horizontal overflow position after the first animation frame.
- Generic date selection could move an unfinished workout and its completed sets to another date.

FIXES
- Today starts on the device-local current date after every normal app launch.
- An active workout keeps its original date so unsaved sets are preserved.
- Today explicitly resets selectedWorkoutDate, calendarViewDate and persisted selection.
- Calendar centering is applied after the first paint and repeated after iOS layout/scroll restoration.
- Programmatic centering cannot be overwritten by its own scroll event.
- A workout already running on another date must be finished or deleted; its entries are never copied to Today.
- After that old workout is finished or deleted, the screen automatically returns to the real Today date.
