# v202: progressive flexibility prescriptions

The back/hip plan remains an individually assembled routine, not an association-approved program. ACSM's 2011 position stand recommends about 60 seconds accumulated per flexibility exercise on at least two days weekly (PMID 21694556). Ingram et al. 2025 reviewed 189 studies: regular static stretching improves range, but greater intensity was not associated with greater improvement and benefits did not continue increasing indefinitely with volume (PMID 39614059). These findings do not identify a universally optimal routine or diagnose stiffness.

The guide now offers explicit Start (1×20 seconds per side) and Develop (2×30 seconds per side) buttons. These apply to the three existing hip flexor, hamstring and glute holds, in the selected gym or home program only. No automatic progression. All gym templates now include these holds after movement drills; existing customized layouts remain intact. Easy-day tension stays mild; reduce the dose if next-day symptoms worsen. The guide distinguishes movement/control exercises from static stretching, includes a two-week comparison protocol, and links the research directly.

Prescription changes are blocked during any active workout and preserve exercise selection, order, slots, unrelated settings, completed logs and history. No new storage schema or account/backend change. Timer defaults follow the visible prescription. Tests cover both levels, invalid levels, active-session guard and preservation of customized layouts in addition to the existing mobility/session/save/delete regressions.
